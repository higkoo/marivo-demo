# What changed in the passenger charge composition?

Compare monthly `mobility.total_charged`, `mobility.fare_revenue`,
`mobility.total_surcharges`, `mobility.tips`, and `mobility.tolls` during 2025.
Decompose surcharges into extras, MTA tax, improvement surcharge, NYS congestion
surcharge, airport fees, and CBD congestion fees.

Pay particular attention to the January 5 start of the CBD fee, segment by
rate code and pickup service zone, and retain correction-like records in the
published totals while assessing their quality impact.
