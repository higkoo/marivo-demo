# What changed in the passenger charge composition?

Track monthly passenger charges, metered fares, surcharges, recorded tips, and
tolls during 2025. Break surcharges into extras, MTA tax, improvement surcharge,
NYS congestion surcharge, airport fees, and CBD congestion fees.

Pay particular attention to changes around the January 5 start of the CBD fee,
and compare them across rate categories and pickup service zones. Keep published
totals intact, but show whether correction-like or implausible records materially
change the conclusion.
